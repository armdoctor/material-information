The economics of AI infrastructure, read from the documents.

Armand Mohan · Singapore · 2026

The AI buildout is a supply chain: power, land, chips, capital and permits, each with its own bottleneck. Most writing about it starts from the narrative. This publication starts from the filings, tenders, indentures and contracts, and works forward from what they actually say. Written from Singapore, with a particular interest in Southeast Asia and in how all of this gets financed.
