What is a megawatt worth in Singapore?

Forthcoming · November 2026 · Armand Mohan

In August 2026, Singapore's Economic Development Board and Infocomm Media Development Authority provisionally allocated 200 megawatts of new data centre capacity, 50MW each to Digital Realty, Equinix, Keppel and ST Telemedia, from a field of more than twenty proposals. The sites are on Jurong Island. The conditions include more than half of power from green sources, liquid cooling throughout, and Green Mark Platinum certification.

Singapore does not ration data centre capacity by price. It rations it by allocation. That makes the award a rare, legible instance of a government deciding who gets a scarce input in the AI supply chain, and on what terms. This essay works through what the four winners actually received: what a 50MW licence permits, what the green-power and efficiency conditions are likely to cost, how each operator will pay for it based on what their public filings disclose, and how the resulting cost per kilowatt-hour on Jurong Island compares with the gigawatts being built across the Causeway in Johor.

The question it tries to answer is whether the winners got a prize or a burden.
